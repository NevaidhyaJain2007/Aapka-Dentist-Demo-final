GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

Need something else? Ask the virtual receptionist anytime.

When is the clinic open?＋

Demo hours are Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–6 PM in the configured clinic time zone.

What services can I ask about?＋

Consultations for general dentistry, cleaning, fillings, root canal, extraction, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, and urgent dental assessment.

How do I book an appointment?＋

Open the receptionist and choose “Book a visit.” We’ll show real available demo schedule times and save your booking.

Can I cancel an appointment?＋

Yes. Provide the booking reference and email used, review the appointment, then confirm cancellation.

What should I do in a dental emergency?＋

For facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek urgent professional care immediately.
